Editorial Standards

Accuracy and verification

EP News aims to verify consequential claims using original documents, official records, and independent reporting wherever possible. Unverified claims should be identified as such.

Reporting and analysis

News reporting should distinguish verified facts from interpretation, opinion, and speculation. Headlines should accurately reflect the evidence presented.

Corrections

Readers can report errors to eyepokernews@gmail.com. Substantiated errors should be corrected transparently.

Sources

We aim to attribute material claims and link supporting sources where practicable. We do not treat a single partisan account as independent verification.
