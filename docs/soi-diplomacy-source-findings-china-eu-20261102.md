# Source-checked diplomatic evidence: China and EU

## China: Saudi–Iran rapprochement

The signed [Saudi-hosted publication of the trilateral statement](https://www.spa.gov.sa/en/ed7aabfefaw) and [Chinese foreign ministry reproduction](https://www.mfa.gov.cn/eng/wjb/zzjg_663340/xybfs_663590/xwlb_663592/202303/t20230311_11039241.html) identify China as host and sponsor of the March 6–10, 2023 Beijing talks. Importantly, the signatories themselves credit **Iraq and Oman** for dialogue rounds in 2021–22. This is verified evidence of China's hosting role and explicit evidence against a China-only mediation narrative. It does **not** measure China's incremental causal contribution, security outcomes or costs. Attribution and cost gates remain open.

## EU: Moldova mission financial reference amounts

[Consolidated EU Council Decision 2023/855](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/PDF/?uri=CELEX%3A02023D0855-20250520) and [amendment 2025/943](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=celex%3A32025D0943) specify nominal EUPM Moldova financial reference amounts: EUR 13,356,508.08 for 24 April 2023–21 May 2025 and EUR 19,817,594.70 for 22 May 2025–31 May 2027, a nominal difference of EUR 6,461,086.62. These are **legal budget reference amounts**, not verified actual expenditure, disbursement, measurable security performance or a net cost-benefit estimate. The mission was created before the partnership. Attribution and cost gates remain open.

Both cases remain unscored. No influence needle or UI changes.
