Official source: [EIA U.S. Natural Gas Prices](https://www.eia.gov/dnav/ng/NG_PRI_SUM_DCU_NUS_M.htm). Nominal USD per thousand cubic feet; July 2025 vs July 2026.

| Sector | July 2025 | July 2026 | Difference |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Industrial | $4.64 | $4.58 | -$0.06 (-1.29%) |
| Residential | $25.44 | $26.45 | +$1.01 (+3.97%) |

These are national delivered price averages, **not** a matched H1 2025/2026 comparison, and not an export-caused price estimate. Retail residential delivered prices include distribution and customer mix effects; seasonality and weather matter. The industrial national average need not reflect any particular plant's price.
