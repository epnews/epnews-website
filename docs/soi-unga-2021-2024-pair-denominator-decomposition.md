# Decomposing 2021–24 UN voting matched-pair changes

Matched voting pairs change because both **actor-eligible resolution counts** and **observed partner pairs per eligible resolution** change. The following is an exact arithmetic decomposition using 2021's observed pair rate as the baseline, not a causal attribution.

| Sphere | Total matched-pair change | Eligible-resolution count component | Pairs-per-resolution component |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| US | +1567 | 1305.5 | 261.5 |
| CHINA | +1415 | 1159.2 | 255.8 |
| RUSSIA | +1571 | 1304.1 | 266.9 |
| INDIA | +1676 | 1454.5 | 221.5 |
| EU | +853 | 577.3 | 275.7 |

**Identity:** Δ pairs = (Δ eligible resolutions × 2021 pairs per eligible resolution) + (2024 eligible resolutions × Δ pairs per eligible resolution). The allocation between components depends on the baseline-year convention; it does not isolate causal mechanisms. Changing agenda composition, validity rules, and partner participation remain potential explanations. The EU is a majority-voting proxy. These figures do not support a geopolitical influence score or moving the command-meter needle.
