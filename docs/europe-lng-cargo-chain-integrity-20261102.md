# LNG cargo chain-of-evidence screening

The screening requires plausible loading ≤ arrival ≤ discharge dates, positive discharge volume, an explicit LNG measurement unit, and a seven-digit IMO *format*. These are syntactic research triage gates, **not** proof of source authenticity, IMO checksum validity, actual vessel movement, cargo identity, physical production origin, or geopolitical influence. Validate against primary terminal and genuinely independent vessel/port records. Normalize units only with sourced conversion assumptions; do not silently mix tonnes LNG, cubic metres LNG, and MMBtu. Missing evidence remains unknown.
