# Five-sphere diplomatic attribution and cost evidence gates

This is a **research execution register**, not a finding of causal influence. The existing five preselected case files each have four completed source/baseline/follow-up tasks and two unresolved tasks: attribution and costs. No additional task is marked complete by this bundle.

## US — 2023 Camp David trilateral cooperation commitments

**Attribution alternatives:** Pre-existing US-Japan and US-ROK defense alliances; Japan-ROK GSOMIA restoration and independent reconciliation; Shared DPRK missile threat.

**Evidence needed to distinguish explanations:** Dated trilateral negotiation records showing unique US intervention; Independent Japan/ROK operational data showing new exchange performance; Counterfactual chronology against pre-summit arrangements.

**Costs and concessions to measure:** Incremental integration and maintenance spending by all three governments; Technology and information-sharing concessions; Operational burden and cybersecurity safeguards.

**Gate state:** attribution unverified; costs unverified; influence score not authorized.

## CHINA — 2023 Beijing agreement to resume Saudi-Iran diplomatic relations

**Attribution alternatives:** Earlier Iraq and Oman mediation; Saudi and Iranian bilateral security/economic interests; Regional de-escalation incentives independent of Beijing.

**Evidence needed to distinguish explanations:** Negotiating records distinguishing Beijing's incremental role from prior talks; Independent counterpart accounts of concessions caused by Chinese intervention; Durability and security outcomes relative to earlier mediation.

**Costs and concessions to measure:** Documented Chinese mediation resources and guarantees; Saudi/Iranian concessions and compliance costs; Regional security outcomes and reversals.

**Gate state:** attribution unverified; costs unverified; influence score not authorized.

## RUSSIA — Russia-Belarus Union State security guarantees treaty

**Attribution alternatives:** Pre-existing Union State and CSTO obligations; Belarusian government strategic choices; Regional security escalation and sanctions.

**Evidence needed to distinguish explanations:** Treaty article-by-article incremental obligations versus earlier agreements; Belarusian ratification debate and independently verified implementation; Counterfactual security arrangements absent 2025 treaty.

**Costs and concessions to measure:** Financing and military commitments by each state; Belarusian sovereignty and basing concessions; Sanctions exposure and security externalities.

**Gate state:** attribution unverified; costs unverified; influence score not authorized.

## EU — EU–Moldova Security and Defence Partnership

**Attribution alternatives:** Russia's invasion of Ukraine and Moldovan threat perceptions; Moldova's domestic EU-integration decisions; EUPM mission established before partnership.

**Evidence needed to distinguish explanations:** Specific implemented partnership deliverables distinct from EUPM baseline; Moldovan agency reporting and independent outcome audits; Evidence separating EU inducements from Moldova's autonomous preferences.

**Costs and concessions to measure:** EU commitments versus appropriations versus actual disbursements; Moldovan co-financing and administrative burdens; Mission operating costs and documented outcomes.

**Gate state:** attribution unverified; costs unverified; influence score not authorized.

## INDIA — India-UAE Comprehensive Economic Partnership Agreement enters into force

**Attribution alternatives:** Oil price and energy import movements; Post-pandemic trade rebound and exchange-rate shifts; UAE demand and non-CEPA policy changes.

**Evidence needed to distinguish explanations:** Product-level tariff-line counterfactual with untreated comparator markets; Volume versus price decomposition by product; UAE and India customs data matched by fiscal period and valuation.

**Costs and concessions to measure:** Tariff revenue forgone by partner and year; Domestic sector adjustment costs; Distributional gains and losses and compliance costs.

**Gate state:** attribution unverified; costs unverified; influence score not authorized.

## Comparable decision rules

Use dated primary and independent counterpart sources, consistent units and matched baseline/follow-up windows. Do not equate an announcement, signed agreement, mission budget reference, or observed trade increase with an attributable influence gain. Unknown costs are not zero. Track net effects and reversals before any scoring proposal. The original needle and all selectors remain unchanged.
