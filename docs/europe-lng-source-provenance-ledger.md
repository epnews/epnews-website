## Anti-double-counting rule
NUPI's report and Bruegel's dataset describe **one underlying import series**, not two independent observations. The US+Russia 81% share is an arithmetic composition of the same source, not another finding. CSIS risk scores are modeled metrics, not additional import measurements. The EU recommendation is one legal event, even if many news articles discuss it.

## Acceptance rule for Europe influence dial
A supplier-exposure indicator may be displayed with its own geography, period and data lineage after raw Bruegel reproduction. A geopolitical influence score requires dated demands, actual policy decisions, independent corroboration and an assessment of competing causes. Until then **readiness 53% and influence null**.
