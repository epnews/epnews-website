# Keep UN voting agreement separate from data coverage

Four-year **pooled** agreement uses actual observed partner-vote pairs as its denominator. Partner-pair coverage instead uses the theoretical ceiling of 160 frozen external partners per actor-eligible resolution. These answer different questions and should not be conflated.

| Actor | Pooled agreement | Partner-pair coverage | Matched pairs |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| US | 27.38% | 91.02% | 44711 |
| CHINA | 67.55% | 90.99% | 44548 |
| RUSSIA | 53.86% | 90.98% | 44983 |
| INDIA | 64.96% | 91.18% | 44788 |
| EU | 56.84% | 90.77% | 38922 |

Pooled agreement is **not** the arithmetic mean of annual agreement percentages. The EU is a majority-vote proxy, not a sovereign actor. Similar voting does not demonstrate coercion, persuasion, economic leverage or policy implementation. No scoring, dial activation or influence-needle change is authorized.

Sources: `data/soi-unga-six-code-restricted-results-2021-2024.json` and `data/soi-unga-four-year-denominator-diagnostics-2021-2024.json`.
