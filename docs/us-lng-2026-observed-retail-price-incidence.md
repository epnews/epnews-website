# Observed US natural-gas delivered prices and electricity pass-through, March–August 2026

**New source, not a repeat of PR #258:** EIA Natural Gas Monthly released **October 30, 2026**, [US Natural Gas Summary](https://www.eia.gov/dnav/ng/ng_sum_lsum_dcu_nus_m.htm) lists **actual reported monthly prices paid by end-use sectors** and corresponding sectoral gas deliveries. PR #258 previously captured Henry Hub summer spot prices, production and storage but did not record these delivered price series.

These are **nominal dollars per thousand cubic feet (Mcf)**, not Henry Hub **$/MMBtu**; converting requires a documented heat-content factor. Residential summer unit prices may be higher because fixed charges are spread across much lower summer gas consumption and the reported mix of customers changes; **March-to-August residential price increases are not evidence of LNG-caused household cost inflation**.

**EIA August 2026 delivered volumes:** residential 103,242 million cubic feet; commercial 149,508; industrial 701,697; electric power 1,556,093. Residential March volume 481,026 million cubic feet versus August 103,242 illustrates why summer residential unit prices cannot be interpreted without volume and billing composition. These are gas deliveries, not consumer bills and not all sectors' expenditures.

## Separate electricity customer price observation
[EIA Electric Power Monthly, August 2026 Table ES1.A](https://www.eia.gov/electricity/monthly/epm_table_grapher.php?t=epmt_es1a): August residential electricity average **18.16 cents/kWh** versus **17.57** in August 2025 (**+3.4%**); commercial **14.47** vs **13.87** (+4.3%); industrial **9.64** vs **8.98** (+7.3%). EIA's reported percentage changes are authoritative; rounding the displayed prices may yield slightly different percentages. Electricity prices incorporate generation mix, transmission, distribution, contracts, weather, fuel costs and regulatory changes; **cannot assign these differences to LNG export demand**.

## Reconciliation and limits
1. PR #258's June–August Henry Hub **$2.93/MMBtu** wholesale summer mean cannot be subtracted from August residential **$27.05/Mcf** as though they were comparable units, periods or delivered products.
2. Do not infer August-versus-August 2025 natural-gas retail changes from March–August 2026 series; same-month 2025 end-user price points must be separately retrieved for YoY comparisons.
3. EIA price × EIA sector volume would yield an **illustrative aggregate reported transaction value** under a consistent customer coverage and units check, **not incremental LNG-caused household costs**. Do not calculate national benefit or gas utility profit from such products.
4. A causal 2026 household burden requires matched pre-shock baseline, regional retail rates/bills, weather and volumes, utility contract lags, production and storage, and a validated no-shock gas price counterfactual.

**Evidence classification:** Newly verified *observed sectoral delivered prices and electricity customer price changes*; **2026 shock-attributable marginal domestic costs remain unresolved**. **Readiness stays 53%; net national benefit and geopolitical influence score null.** No Command Meter code or controls changed.
