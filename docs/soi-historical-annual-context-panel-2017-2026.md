# Historical annual diplomatic context panel, 2017–2026

This reproducible panel extracts the **existing** four historical event registers into 50 actor-year context rows (five spheres × ten years), preserving canonical event identifiers, date, source lead and stage. It removes one known duplicate of the 2026 US Paris Agreement withdrawal effective milestone.

**Important:** `actors` means the sphere was listed as relevant context in the source register. It does **not** imply the actor initiated the event, benefited, exercised diplomatic influence, or scored a success. NATO accession, war, sanctions, trade agreements and treaty withdrawal must not all be called diplomacy outcomes. For that reason all event categories remain `historical-context-unclassified`, attributed outcomes and scores are null, and the annual event counts are explicitly non-scoring research inventory counts.
