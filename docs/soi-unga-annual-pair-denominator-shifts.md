# Annual denominator shifts in UN voting comparisons

Year-over-year agreement percentages use **different counts of matched partner-vote pairs** and different resolutions. This audit exposes denominator changes before interpreting apparent trends.

| Actor | 2021 pairs | 2024 pairs | Pair change | 2021 pairs / eligible resolution | 2024 pairs / eligible resolution |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| US | 10589 | 12156 | +1567 | 145.05 | 148.24 |
| CHINA | 10723 | 12138 | +1415 | 144.91 | 148.02 |
| RUSSIA | 10723 | 12294 | +1571 | 144.91 | 148.12 |
| INDIA | 10618 | 12294 | +1676 | 145.45 | 148.12 |
| EU | 9381 | 10234 | +853 | 144.32 | 148.32 |

The change in pair counts reflects both the number of eligible resolutions and valid matched partner votes; it cannot by itself identify which mechanism dominates. These are denominator diagnostics, not evidence of diplomacy causing changed voting behavior. No influence scoring or needle changes.

Source: `data/soi-unga-six-code-restricted-results-2021-2024.json`.
