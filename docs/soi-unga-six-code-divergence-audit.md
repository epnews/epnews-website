# UN voting agreement: full agenda versus six-code sensitivity audit

The following differences are **six-code subset minus full-agenda voting agreement**, in percentage points. This is a descriptive sampling sensitivity check, not a geopolitical influence score.

| Sphere | Mean absolute gap (pp), 2021–24 | Maximum absolute gap (pp) | Gap changes sign? |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| United States | 3.64 | 4.45 | No |
| China | 2.07 | 3.01 | Yes |
| Russia | 2.71 | 2.82 | Yes |
| India | 1.33 | 3.64 | Yes |
| EU voting-majority proxy | 3.02 | 4.52 | No |

## Method

For each actor and year, calculate `100 × agreements / matched-country-vote-pairs` from the restricted dataset, then subtract the frozen full-agenda agreement percentage. The six-code subset (102, 110, 111, 112, 118, 129) was selected **after** examining annual resolution support and is not a representative or preregistered sample. Its eligible resolutions per year are 74, 77, 75, 83. The EU actor is a 75%-majority EU27 voting proxy with a minimum of 20 member votes, not a sovereign state. Vote pairs within resolutions are dependent. No causal or influence-score inference is justified.

## Decision

Differences are nonzero and vary by actor and year. The subset is suitable for a visibly labeled sensitivity comparison, not replacement of the full baseline, scoring, or automatic needle movement. The 19 issue-uncoded resolutions remain a separate topic-standardization coverage gap.
