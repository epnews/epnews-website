## Finance Ministry fiscal receipts: independently reported actual budget series

Source: [Reuters' October 6 reproduction of Russian Finance Ministry September 2026 data](https://ru.themoscowtimes.com/2026/10/06/tablitsa-neftegazovye-dokhody-rossiyskogo-byudzheta-za-sentyabr-2026-goda-a207874); [Russian Finance Ministry budget statistics portal](https://minfin.gov.ru/en/statistics/fedbud/). Units below are **billion Russian rubles**, nominal, federal oil-and-gas budget revenue, not gross energy sales or corporate net income.

| Crude mineral extraction tax | 592.6 | 688.3 | Tax component, **not the same as net budget revenue** |

| Refinery price-damper budget compensation | −197.3 | −305.5 | Negative fiscal line (refinery subsidy mechanism), not a company export cost |

For January–September 2026, federal oil-and-gas budget receipts totaled **5,471.1 billion RUB**, **17.2% lower** than January–September 2025. The September 2025 monthly comparator is approximately 582.5 billion RUB (September 2026 plus reported 130.1 billion RUB year-over-year decline).

**Critical reconciliation:** The September 688.3 billion RUB crude MET is greater than 452.4 billion RUB net federal oil-and-gas receipts because the budget series incorporates multiple tax categories and negative offset/subsidy lines. Do not interpret the difference as an extraction cost, operating loss, profit margin, or cash leakage. Fiscal receipts are observed/reported government collections, but still do not determine national net economic benefit or attributable conflict gains.

## Evidence reconciliation

1. CREA's September gross export sales and September crude MET are different economic concepts: sales and a tax collection. Do **not** subtract the tax from export revenue to derive profit, or add it to sales as another export benefit.
2. KSE's August oil-only model and CREA's August all-fossil model differ in product scope, currency and methodology. Do **not** equate their totals without a documented reconciliation.
3. KSE's US$4.4B August modeled sanctions-related revenue loss is **not** an actual expenditure. Do not subtract it from CREA's gross revenue or treat it as a net loss.
4. The August-to-September CREA daily rates are estimates subject to revisions; volume, price, destination, exchange-rate and loading/unloading timing effects require separate decomposition.
5. The historical decline in EU gas purchases does not imply worldwide Russian export revenue declined by the same amount. China, India, Turkiye and other buyers must be included.

## Missing evidence before any benefit claim

- Verified destination-matched contract prices, actual collections and payment delays.
- Extraction and refining costs, freight, insurance, tanker acquisition and sanctions-evasion costs.
- Independent fiscal collections from Russia's finance ministry with tax base and timing reconciliation.
- Realistic no-disruption/no-sanctions counterfactual and incremental impact on Russian consumers and public spending.
- Consistent monthly series and product/coverage reconciliation across CREA and KSE.

**Meter behavior:** keep the existing September 2026 CREA €625m/day observed needle and `score:null`. This evidence milestone strengthens the supporting panel and sources; it does not justify a net benefit, influence score, or increase to the 53% investigation-readiness estimate.
