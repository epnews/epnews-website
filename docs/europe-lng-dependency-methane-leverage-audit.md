# Europe LNG dependence and EU methane-regulation response: cross-source evidence audit

**Scope:** Evidence newly extracted from NUPI/Bruegel, EU primary legal text, CSIS, Oxford Institute for Energy Studies and Baker Institute. Does not recycle US export-price, corporate-revenue or domestic gas-cost findings from PRs #248–259.

## 1. Measured European LNG supplier concentration
NUPI's 2026 analysis, [Europe's Diversification Paradox](https://www.geopol.no/en/publications/europe-s-diversification-paradox-growing-us-and-russian-gas-import-shares-in-2026), draws on [Bruegel's European natural-gas import tracker](https://www.bruegel.org/dataset/european-natural-gas-imports) and reports first-half LNG shares:
| Supplier | H1 2025 | H1 2026 | Change |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| United States | 57% | 62% | +5 percentage points |
| Russia | 16% | 19% | +3 percentage points |
| All others, residual | 27% | 19% | -8 percentage points |

**Denominator:** EEA **LNG imports**, not all natural-gas imports, EU-only imports, total energy, or US LNG export destinations. The residual is derived as 100% minus the reported two suppliers and inherits rounding and classification limits. Shares are volume-based as reported by the analyst; a higher share does not necessarily mean higher absolute volumes. The sum of squares of these *three buckets* would be a misleading concentration index because the residual combines multiple independent countries; **do not compute HHI from this collapsed table**.

The 62% US LNG share establishes **supplier exposure**, not an American contractual veto, inability to switch suppliers, a realized profit or a diplomatic concession. The reported US+Russia shares total 81% of LNG in H1 2026, but they are **two different counterparties** and should not be combined into a single geopolitical influence score.

## 2. EU methane-rule legal action: recommendation versus repeal
**Primary text:** [European Commission recommendation on enforcement of Regulation (EU) 2024/1787, EUR-Lex](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reco/2026/1660/oj/eng), published 2026. It recommends that member states refrain from applying certain penalties for importers' methane-related reporting obligations due in **2027–2029**, subject to exceptions including fraud. The **recommendation is not legally binding**. It does **not** amend, repeal or suspend the underlying Methane Regulation or prevent national regulators from applying their legal powers.

**Pressure chronology:** NUPI reports US ambassador public pressure on April 27, 2026 and later Commission enforcement-flexibility recommendation. Industry groups, Qatar and member states also sought flexibility amid supply-security concerns. A public demand followed by a nonbinding policy accommodation is a **documented sequence**, not proof that LNG dependency caused the recommendation. Verify any claim about national implementation separately using national regulator records.

**Causal grading:**
- **GREEN:** existence, text and nonbinding status of EU recommendation; observed NUPI/Bruegel reported LNG shares.
- **YELLOW:** whether US lobbying was materially influential versus Qatar, member states, energy-security shocks and implementation burden.
- **RED/UNPROVEN:** assertion that Washington compelled Europe to change the law or that a particular LNG contract produced a diplomatic concession.

## 3. Independent models and counterarguments
**CSIS:** [Beyond Russian Gas: Trade-Offs in EU LNG Diversification](https://www.csis.org/analysis/beyond-russian-gas-trade-offs-eu-liquefied-natural-gas-diversification) separates portfolio supplier concentration, geopolitical risk and price volatility; its modeled supplier-risk numbers (e.g. US 1.21, Qatar 1.75 under the report's assumptions) are **not directly comparable to EP News's geopolitical influence dial**. The paper's pre-disruption modeling baseline is useful for sensitivity checks, not an observed 2026 US concession estimate.

**Oxford Institute for Energy Studies:** [Geopolitics of Gas: The Limits of Leverage](https://www.oxfordenergy.org/wpcms/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Insight-173-Geopolitics-of-Gas-The-Limits-of-Leverage.pdf) examines how supply coercion can trigger diversification, demand reduction or alternative infrastructure. It is a **counter-hypothesis** to assuming that supplier concentration creates lasting leverage. Its China/Russia pipeline possibilities should not be scored as commissioned flows.

**Baker Institute:** [Why Is Europe Not Replacing Russian Pipeline Gas With Long-term LNG Contracts?](https://www.bakerinstitute.org/research/why-europe-not-replacing-russian-pipeline-gas-long-term-lng-contracts) considers contract duration, spot-market risk and infrastructure. Historical estimates of possible contract-related savings are **modeled counterfactuals**; do not import them as realized 2026 EU savings or US economic gain.

**Columbia Center on Global Energy Policy:** [The Geopolitical Weaponisation of LNG](https://www.energypolicy.columbia.edu/publications/the-geopolitical-weaponisation-of-lng/) is retained as a conceptual lead, **not used for new numeric claims** because the full underlying article was not accessible during the review.

## 4. Specific tests before dial activation or readiness uplift
1. Reproduce US/Russia shares from raw Bruegel H1 2025 and H1 2026 LNG import country rows, including EEA/EU geography, shipment classification and revisions. NUPI's published shares are **secondary-source measurements** until independently rebuilt.
2. Read the EUR-Lex recommendation's exact clauses and verify which importer reporting deadlines and penalties are covered, and whether member states implemented the recommendation.
3. Match dated US/Qatar/industry requests to Commission impact documents and meeting records; distinguish lobbying from decisive causation.
4. Match European buyers' contract flexibility, destination restrictions and terminal utilization before asserting coercive dependence.
5. Keep supplier exposure, modeled risk and realized policy concessions as **separate dimensions**. Never transfer CSIS 1.21 risk number to an EP News needle without methodological equivalence.

## Decision
This package supplies a **new observed supplier-share comparison** and **documented EU legal-response event** with explicit source and causation boundaries. Neither proves US national net benefit or a shock-attributable diplomatic concession. **Readiness remains 53%; net national benefit null; geopolitical influence score null.** No existing Command Meter code, selectors, comparisons or needles changed.
