# Cheniere issuer financial evidence — first realized economic milestone

Source: [Cheniere H1 2026 earnings release](https://lngir.cheniere.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/343/cheniere-reports-second-quarter-2026-results-and-raises) and [SEC 2026 Q2 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/3570/000000357026000028/lng-20260630.htm). All financials are consolidated company-wide, not EU-only, and in USD millions.

| Metric | H1 2025 | H1 2026 | Change |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Total revenue | 10,085 | 11,600 | +1,515 (+15.0%) |
| LNG revenue | 9,820 | 11,362 | +1,542 (+15.7%) |
| Consolidated adjusted EBITDA (non-GAAP) | 3,288 | 4,137 | +849 (+25.8%) |
| Net income (GAAP) | +1,979 | -434 | -2,413 |
| LNG exported (TBtu) | 1,159 | 1,360 | +201 (+17.3%) |
| Cargoes exported | 322 | 371 | +49 |

Issuer attributes EBITDA increase to higher delivered LNG volumes and higher per-MMBtu margins. Net income is materially affected by mark-to-market derivative fair-value changes and should not be used as a clean operating cash-flow proxy.

**Evidence gained:** a major U.S. LNG exporter reported a $849m increase in H1 adjusted EBITDA. **Not demonstrated:** this increase was caused by geopolitical disruptions, represents realized profit attributable to EU customers, or equals a net national economic benefit. The company is one issuer, not all U.S. LNG exporters. Adjusted EBITDA is not cash receipts or net profit. DOE FOB prices include feedgas/liquefaction up to point of export and are not margins.
