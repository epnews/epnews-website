# H1 2025–2026 U.S. gas price-volume incidence audit

Inputs: verified official EIA monthly residential and industrial delivered prices (PR #287), and H1 sector deliveries, dry gas production and LNG exports (PR #290). This is an **illustrative sensitivity calculation**, not a measured domestic gas bill or an estimate of LNG-export-caused costs.

For each sector, calculate arithmetic mean of six monthly nominal prices (USD/Mcf). Multiply that mean by total H1 deliveries (Bcf) to form a **hypothetical gross-value proxy in million USD**. Exact algebraic decomposition: price effect = (P26−P25)×Q25; volume effect = (Q26−Q25)×P25; interaction = (P26−P25)×(Q26−Q25). These sum to the proxy difference. None is a causal export effect.

**Critical coverage gap:** EIA average retail prices cover onsystem sales, not all transportation-only or marketer-supplied gas included in total sector deliveries. Monthly arithmetic mean is not consumption weighted. Thus the proxy cannot be described as actual residential or industrial expenditure, revenue, or bills. Price effect can reflect weather, fuel market, contracts, infrastructure and many other factors.

Additional research needed before publishing real sector spending: monthly onsystem sales volumes matched to prices, retail revenue if available, and actual consumer expenditure coverage. To estimate LNG export incidence: a documented counterfactual using supply/demand responses, weather, production, storage, pipelines and international markets.
