# Command Meter: cross-country investigation reset

## Why this milestone
The DOE/Eurostat LNG work established an important **measurement-definition** problem, not an independently measured U.S. economic windfall or a causal diplomatic concession. Verified DOE EU27 destination exports H1 2025 ~42.11 bcm, H1 2026 ~43.36 bcm; Eurostat US-partner imports ~25.84 and ~29.18 bcm. The 14–16 bcm gap is a reporting/attribution question. **Pause further customs-code work unless it materially blocks a causal or cost finding.**

## Five-country comparison
Research gates are in `data/command-meter-cross-country-research-gates.json`: US, Europe, China, Russia, India. For each, prioritize (1) who captured realized revenue/margins or bore costs, (2) disruption-attributable increment against a pre-specified counterfactual, (3) dated and independently verifiable changes in another actor's policy or bargaining behavior. Each country uses the same standards; no score from trade volumes or media claims alone.

## Priority sequence
1. U.S. LNG realized producer/exporter revenue and net margin, plus household/industry energy price effects. DOE FOB prices are prices, **not realized profits**.
2. Europe importer costs and energy resilience versus counterfactual suppliers; test whether any concrete concession followed U.S. energy reliance.
3. Russia export cash receipts after discounts/logistics/sanctions costs, plus military/fiscal offsets.
4. India discount pass-through to refiners, consumers and treasury, not just gross imports.
5. China discounted energy/critical-resource economic incidence and verifiable foreign-policy outcomes.

## Scoring discipline
Existing meter editorial readiness **53%** is a planning estimate. Existing influence and net-national-benefit fields remain null. Cross-country research gates are an **investigation backlog**, not a new scoring model or replacement of existing active dials. Keep moving needle, selectors, comparisons and evidence panels unchanged.

## Definition of next measurable advance
At least one source-backed **realized cost or margin** comparison with matching periods and units, plus a documented causal limitation; or one independently verified specific policy outcome with plausible alternatives evaluated. Research gate validation passing does not itself justify increasing readiness.
