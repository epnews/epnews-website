# SOI US–Japan–ROK missile-warning implementation: evidence boundary (2026-11-02)

## Independently opened source records

- **Before 2023:** A US DoD account dated August 15, 2022 describes Japan, ROK and US sharing tactical data-link information during the August 8–14 Pacific Dragon missile-warning exercise. This prevents the false baseline that trilateral sharing started from zero in December 2023. [DoD](https://www.defense.gov/News/Releases/Release/Article/3128442/)
- **December 19, 2023:** The joint ministerial statement, published by Japan's Ministry of Defense, says the real-time DPRK missile-warning mechanism was fully activated following tests of operational capability. It does not report event-by-event latency, detection sensitivity, or operational benefit. [Japan MOD](https://www.mod.go.jp/en/article/2023/12/67e3c202e8d82c24e6ec3f85308f233509da8c35.html)
- **June 27–29, 2024:** The first Freedom Edge multi-domain exercise began, with participating ships and aircraft named and ballistic missile defense among training domains. [US Pacific Fleet](https://www.cpf.navy.mil/Newsroom/News/Article/3820138/trilateral-statement-first-execution-of-multi-domain-japan-rok-us-exercise-free/)
- **July 28, 2024:** The three defense ministers signed a framework for institutionalizing trilateral security cooperation. The framework is not itself a measured deterrence outcome. [Japan MOD](https://www.mod.go.jp/en/article/2024/07/67ea7647966dfa85d96fb0d25deacf3ce69eedb0.html)
- **November 13, 2024:** The second Freedom Edge iteration began. The existence of two named exercises in 2024 is measurable, but cannot be used as a proxy for missile warning quality. [US 7th Fleet](https://www.c7f.navy.mil/media/news/display/article/3964360/japan-rok-us-conduct-second-exercise-freedom-edge/)
- **Late 2024:** A US defense official described the warning mechanism as used 'on a regular basis' in an official speech. This is a qualitative participant assertion, not an independently audited use count. [DoD](https://www.defense.gov/News/Speeches/Speech/Article/3983625/principal-deputy-assistant-secretary-of-defense-for-indo-pacific-security-affai/)
- **September 5, 2025:** Japan's Joint Staff announced a third Freedom Edge exercise planned for September 15–19, with approximately **700 Japanese personnel**. This is a *plan*, not independently verified attendance or the combined force size. [Japan Joint Staff PDF](https://www.mod.go.jp/js/pdf/2025/p20250905_01e.pdf)

## What this does and does not establish

The before/after series supports a limited **institutional implementation** finding: trilateral missile-warning exercises predated 2023, a continuous sharing mechanism was officially activated in December 2023, and two named multi-domain exercise iterations commenced in 2024. Official records from Japan and the United States provide cross-government corroboration but are not independent performance audits.

The series **does not** establish improved detection or response latency, missile interception success, deterrence, national benefit, costs, or influence over partner decisions. Changes in DPRK launch activity, pre-existing 2014 arrangements and bilateral sharing are confounders. Exercise iteration counts and 2025 planned participants must not be conflated with actual warning-network use.
