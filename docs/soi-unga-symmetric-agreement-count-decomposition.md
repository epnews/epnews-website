# Symmetric UNGA agreement-pair change decomposition

The earlier decomposition used 2021 as a baseline, which can allocate the interaction between sample size and agreement rate differently from a 2024 baseline. This **symmetric midpoint decomposition** averages the two orders, removing that arbitrary baseline-order choice while remaining descriptive.

| Actor | Change in agreement pairs | Symmetric sample-size component | Symmetric agreement-rate component |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| US | +859 | 401.6 | 457.4 |
| CHINA | +898 | 966.7 | -68.7 |
| RUSSIA | +761 | 858.6 | -97.6 |
| INDIA | +1265 | 1086.3 | 178.7 |
| EU | +523 | 478.8 | 44.2 |

For observed agreement pairs A = matched pairs N × agreement fraction p: ΔA = ΔN × (p₀+p₁)/2 + Δp × (N₀+N₁)/2. The two components sum to the exact count difference (subject to displayed rounding). This is an accounting identity, not evidence that sample changes caused diplomatic outcomes. The 2021 and 2024 resolutions are not a matched panel; EU is a voting-majority proxy. No influence score or needle movement is authorized.
