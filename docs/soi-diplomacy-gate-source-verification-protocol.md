## Verification gates

A URL or dated official statement is a lead, not proof of independently measured outcomes. Reviewers must record publication dates, exact claim, baseline/follow-up definition, competing explanation, evidence of counterpart agency, and actual cost unit before changing a gate status. Preserve the command meter and its unscored diplomacy state until calibration is approved.
