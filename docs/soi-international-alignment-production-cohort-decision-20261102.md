# International Alignment — production cohort decision and fail-closed gates

The preferred descriptive reference series is **all recorded UNGA Y/N/A resolutions, 2021–2024, with a frozen external partner cohort of 160 states**, excluding consensus/nonvoting. This is a transparent observational series, **not** an issue-adjusted estimate, causal diplomatic influence, or net national benefit.

## Why we cannot yet treat the trend as issue-adjusted

The existing 60-topic audit shows missing pooled-weight topics for every sphere in every year:

| Sphere | 2021 | 2022 | 2023 | 2024 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| US | 9 | 12 | 7 | 13 |
| CHINA | 9 | 12 | 7 | 13 |
| RUSSIA | 9 | 12 | 7 | 13 |
| INDIA | 9 | 12 | 7 | 13 |
| EU | 13 | 12 | 11 | 15 |

These are counts of **missing topic families**, not missing country votes. The topic-adjusted score stays **null**. Six-code restriction shifts readings by as much as 4.5179 pp. The existing strict EU sensitivity dataset includes only 2021 and 2024 readings, so 2022–23 strict-threshold sensitivity remains open. Worst-case missing-partner-vote bounds are not confidence intervals.

## Publication contract

- A source-labeled **historical descriptive table/chart** may use full-cohort percentages with explicit denominators, coverage, EU-proxy caveat, topic-composition warning, and separate sensitivity panels.
- A **moving International Alignment dial** is still blocked until all-year EU threshold and cohort alternatives are verified and standalone scale/selector tests pass.
- Preserve all old Diplomatic evidence as an unscored archive; never insert alignment percentages into the old 20% diplomatic-influence component or overall influence composite.
- Stage implementation behind a flag; do not merge until four CI checks pass. This PR changes no runtime files.
