# UN voting agreement-pair numerator: count versus rate

The raw count of agreement pairs can rise even when the **percentage agreeing** falls, because the number of observed matched vote pairs changes. This exact 2021-baseline decomposition separates those arithmetic effects.

| Sphere | Agreement-pair change | Matched-pair count component | Agreement-rate component | Agreement % change (pp) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| US | +859 | 370.1 | 488.9 | 4.02 |
| CHINA | +898 | 971.0 | -73.0 | -0.60 |
| RUSSIA | +761 | 865.3 | -104.3 | -0.85 |
| INDIA | +1265 | 1073.2 | 191.8 | 1.56 |
| EU | +523 | 476.9 | 46.1 | 0.45 |

**Identity:** Δ agreement pairs = Δ matched pairs × 2021 agreement fraction + 2024 matched pairs × Δ agreement fraction. Components depend on the reference-year convention. Different resolution agendas, missing partner votes, and the EU voting proxy prevent causal interpretation. Neither counts nor rates measure diplomatic influence, and neither may move the original command-meter needle.

Source: `data/soi-unga-six-code-restricted-results-2021-2024.json`.
