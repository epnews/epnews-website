# Official legal milestones: Paris Agreement and NATO accessions

Five legal milestones are documented from official UN/UNFCCC and NATO sources. These are **legal-status facts**, not measures of diplomatic influence.

## First US Paris Agreement withdrawal effective (2020-11-04)

Official source: https://treaties.un.org/Pages/showDetails.aspx?objid=0800000280458f37

- US: withdrawing-party
- EU: context-not-withdrawing-party

Evidence scope: UN treaty depositary effective legal status.

## US Paris Agreement reentry effective (2021-02-19)

Official source: https://www.un.org/sg/en/content/sg/notes-correspondents/2021-01-20/note-correspondents-the-united-states-return-the-paris-agreement

- US: rejoining-party
- EU: context-not-rejoining-party

## Second US Paris Agreement withdrawal effective (2026-01-27)

Official source: https://unfccc.int/node/61231

- US: withdrawing-party
- EU: context-not-withdrawing-party

Evidence scope: UNFCCC official country status, distinct from parent UNFCCC treaty.

## Finland became NATO member (2023-04-04)

Official source: https://www.nato.int/cps/en/natohq/news_213448.htm

- US: existing-nato-ally-not-accession-subject
- EU: not-nato-institution
- RUSSIA: context-not-accession-party

Evidence scope: NATO official accession date and institutional membership.

## Sweden became NATO member (2024-03-07)

Official source: https://www.nato.int/cps/en/natohq/news_223446.htm

- US: existing-nato-ally-not-accession-subject
- EU: not-nato-institution
- RUSSIA: context-not-accession-party

Evidence scope: NATO official accession date and institutional membership.

The three US Paris Agreement events are distinct: effective withdrawal in 2020, effective reentry in 2021 and a second effective withdrawal in 2026. They are not duplicate entries. The US remained a party to the parent UNFCCC after the 2026 Paris Agreement withdrawal, with a separate future-effective withdrawal notice. Finland and Sweden acceded to **NATO**, not the European Union; NATO's existing US membership is not an independent US accession outcome. None of these facts establishes measurable influence or costs. No UI or scoring change.
