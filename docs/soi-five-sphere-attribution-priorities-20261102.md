# Five-sphere attribution and cost research: next measurable evidence

This is a **targeted operational measurement plan**, not a new claim of verified effects. It consolidates the existing 10 open audit gates into source and metric-specific next steps.

## US: soi-dip-candidate-1

**Starting point:** Trilateral missile-warning sharing began operating December 19, 2023.

**Attribution test:** Compare actual post-activation sharing latency, coverage and reliability against pre-existing bilateral arrangements, controlling for Japan-ROK normalization.

**Cost test:** Find agency budget execution, personnel and integration cost by country; distinguish existing bilateral systems from incremental trilateral spend.

**Measurable outcome:** Quarterly operational data or publicly disclosed exercise/alert exchange counts; unavailable figures remain null.

Counterpart research portals:
- https://www.mod.go.jp/en/
- https://www.mnd.go.kr/mbshome/mbs/mndEN/

## CHINA: soi-dip-candidate-2

**Starting point:** March 2023 Beijing statement names China as host and Iraq/Oman as earlier mediators.

**Attribution test:** Date Saudi-Iran embassy reopening, ambassador exchanges and security incidents; compare with prior Iraq/Oman negotiation timeline and bilateral incentives.

**Cost test:** Identify any public Chinese mediation outlays or commitments and Saudi/Iran implementation costs; do not substitute unknown with zero.

**Measurable outcome:** Dated restoration of diplomatic missions and bilateral exchange, not China's exclusive causation.

Counterpart research portals:
- https://www.spa.gov.sa/en/
- https://www.mfa.gov.ir/

## RUSSIA: soi-dip-candidate-3

**Starting point:** Russia-Belarus security treaty entered into force March 13, 2025.

**Attribution test:** Compare treaty articles with pre-existing Union State/CSTO guarantees and independently documented new basing or deployment obligations.

**Cost test:** Find audited budget allocations, deployments, sanctions effects and Belarusian obligations; distinguish stated commitment from expenditure.

**Measurable outcome:** Incremental binding clauses and verified implementation versus prior treaty baseline.

Counterpart research portals:
- https://pravo.by/
- https://publication.pravo.gov.ru/

## EU: soi-dip-candidate-eu-moldova-2024

**Starting point:** EUPM Moldova financial reference amounts for two separate periods are EUR 13,356,508.08 and EUR 19,817,594.70; these are not actual expenditures.

**Attribution test:** Separate 2023 EUPM pre-partnership operations from specific 2024 partnership deliverables; compare Moldovan agency outcomes and EU commitments.

**Cost test:** Reconcile mission reference budget, appropriations, actual disbursements and Moldovan co-financing for identical periods.

**Measurable outcome:** Delivered training, institutional capacity and documented security outputs with matched period and counterfactual.

Counterpart research portals:
- https://eur-lex.europa.eu/
- https://www.mfa.gov.md/

## INDIA: soi-dip-candidate-5

**Starting point:** India-UAE CEPA entered into force in 2022; aggregate bilateral trade alone does not isolate treaty effect.

**Attribution test:** Build tariff-line treated-versus-untreated comparisons with matched customs periods and control markets, decomposing price and volume.

**Cost test:** Estimate foregone tariff receipts, compliance and sector adjustment costs from fiscal records rather than gross trade values.

**Measurable outcome:** Tariff-line volumes, unit values and customs receipts in matching fiscal years and valuation bases.

Counterpart research portals:
- https://commerce.gov.in/
- https://www.moec.gov.ae/

All attribution and cost gates remain open. No scoring or command-meter UI change is authorized.
