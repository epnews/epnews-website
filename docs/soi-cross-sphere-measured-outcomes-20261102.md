# Cross-sphere measured outcomes: seven distinct series

A unified source for **descriptive** outcome cards, with source pointers to existing verified datasets. This batch does not infer geopolitical influence, merge incompatible units, or change the command-meter needle.

- **NATO member states** (members): 2023-04-04: 31 → 2024-03-07: 32. Membership milestones, not US/EU causal influence or alliance readiness.
- **Finland defense spending share of GDP** (percentage points of GDP): 2022: 1.7 → 2024: 2.4; change 0.7. 2024 estimate; not solely caused by NATO accession.
- **Sweden defense spending share of GDP** (percentage points of GDP): 2022: 1.5 → 2024: 2.1; change 0.6. 2024 estimate; not solely caused by NATO accession.
- **EU Russian-origin hard-coal imports** (million tonnes): 2021: 49 → 2023: 0.8; change -48.2. One underlying Eurostat series, referenced by two workstreams; not a net sanctions benefit or total Russian export loss.
- **US Treasury customs duty receipts** (USD billions nominal per federal fiscal year): 2024: 77.037 → 2025: 194.866; change 117.829. Federal receipts, not tariff-attributable net economic gain.
- **EU outward FDI stock in China** (EUR billions): 2024: 239.3. Stock, not annual flow or CAI-freeze impact.
- **China outward FDI stock in EU** (EUR billions): 2024: 79.8. Stock, not annual flow or CAI-freeze impact.

The EU coal series is referenced in both sanctions and energy workstreams but counted **once**. India needs its own comparable outcomes rather than borrowed data. No causal attribution or cost gate is closed.
