# Cargo evidence review gates

This validator checks only **field completeness and basic formatting** for research queue entries; it does not fetch or authenticate records. A complete entry is marked `manual-source-review-required`, never verified. Check primary terminal records against an independent vessel/cargo source, vessel IMO checksum, dates, units, loading and transshipment chronology, and whether volumes are physical discharge rather than customs attribution. A reported loading port is not sufficient proof of gas production origin.

Do not equate missing observations with zero. Do not add EU27 to member-state figures. Do not infer U.S. geopolitical influence from reported LNG partner-label shifts. No changes to command-meter behavior, SOI audit 20/30 or readiness 53%.
