# US LNG destination receipts: evidence protocol (31 October 2026)

## Research question
Can the observed change in US LNG exports be linked to **actual destination-specific seller receipts**, net incremental costs and ultimately attributable US national benefit? **Not yet established.** This is a reproducible research protocol, not a completed estimate.

## Non-substitution rules
1. DOE destination-specific FOB export prices **cannot** be called realized revenue, cash receipts, profit, or net national benefit.
2. A hypothetical price × volume product, if calculated, must be labeled **hypothetical gross FOB cargo value**, with unit conversions, reporting-period alignment, coverage and valuation limitations disclosed. It is **not** a company income statement.
3. Cheniere consolidated operating less investing cash is an issuer cash-flow bridge, not national benefit; working-capital timing is material.
4. Destination assignment is not automatically final consumption destination. Re-exports and cargo diversions require checking.
5. LNG contract price may be linked to Henry Hub plus a fixed fee, oil indexes, or spot markers. Do not substitute a single benchmark for observed cash realization.
6. Cost incidence must distinguish exporter, purchaser, shipping firm, US consumers and government; do not double count the same expense.
7. Capacity contracted before the 2026 shock cannot automatically be credited to the shock.
8. A policy concession requires independent, dated corroboration and causal linkage, not just increased LNG purchases.
9. Missing observations stay null; do not fill with zero.
10. No readiness percentage increase unless a previously missing evidence gate closes, with recorded source, method and tests.

## Source hierarchy and next retrieval
- [DOE LNG export reports](https://www.energy.gov/fecm/listings/lng-reports): destination-specific reported export prices/volumes, with reporting definition and vintage verified before use.
- [EIA LNG and natural gas](https://www.eia.gov/naturalgas/): capacity, destination and domestic supply/price context.
- [SEC EDGAR](https://www.sec.gov/edgar/search/): issuer and facility financial disclosures, contract liabilities and cash-flow reconciliations.
- [FERC](https://www.ferc.gov/): project approvals and cost/capacity context.

These are retrieval entry points, **not** proof that the needed destination-matched realized receipts are publicly disclosed. The next empirical milestone must collect specific table rows and independently verify them before adding numerical comparisons.
