# Annual UNGA six-code agreement changes: descriptive, not causal

The restricted six-code resolution set covers 2021–24, but its **individual resolutions and issue mix differ by year**. The figures below describe annual changes in agreement with a fixed external-partner cohort, not increases or decreases in geopolitical influence.

| Actor | 2021 | 2022 | 2023 | 2024 | 2021→24 change (pp) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| US | 23.62% | 31.23% | 26.85% | 27.64% | 4.02 |
| CHINA | 68.62% | 67.16% | 66.35% | 68.02% | -0.60 |
| RUSSIA | 55.08% | 54.32% | 51.76% | 54.23% | -0.85 |
| INDIA | 64.03% | 65.70% | 64.38% | 65.59% | 1.56 |
| EU | 55.91% | 59.07% | 55.99% | 56.36% | 0.45 |

The four-year pooled percentage is weighted by matched partner-vote pairs, not a simple average of the four annual percentages. Annual comparisons are **not matched-resolution comparisons**, and partner voting availability can change. The EU remains a threshold-based voting proxy. No statistical significance, causal influence trend, or needle activation is claimed.

Source: `data/soi-unga-six-code-restricted-results-2021-2024.json`.
