These diagnostics are not independent evidence of diplomatic influence. The EU uses a 75% voting-majority proxy with at least 20 valid member votes; its actor-eligible resolution count is not directly equivalent to sovereign-state participation. The theoretical 160-partner ceiling is not the expected count of valid votes. Partner pairs within a resolution are correlated. No scoring or needle movement is authorized.
