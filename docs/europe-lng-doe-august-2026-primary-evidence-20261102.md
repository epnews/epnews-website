# U.S. DOE LNG exports: latest published primary evidence (as of 2026-11-02)

**Primary source:** U.S. Department of Energy, *Natural Gas Imports and Exports Monthly August 2026*, published October 22, 2026: https://www.energy.gov/hgeo/articles/natural-gas-imports-and-exports-monthly-2026 ; direct PDF: https://www.energy.gov/sites/default/files/2026-10/Natural%20Gas%20Imports%20and%20Exports%20Monthly%20August%202026.pdf

## Verified reported aggregate (January–August 2026)

DOE's Table 3b reports **2,332.8 Bcf** of U.S.-produced LNG exports by vessel to the **Europe region** in Jan–Aug 2026, versus **2,351.5 Bcf** in Jan–Aug 2025 (a decline of 18.7 Bcf, approximately 0.8%). It reports **694 European-destination cargo entries** and 15 countries in Jan–Aug 2026. DOE notes some entries can be split cargos; do **not** assume 694 distinct hull voyages. Its Europe region includes **Türkiye**, and is not the same as EU27. Totals can differ from sums due to independent rounding.

For all destinations, DOE reports **4,200.1 Bcf** of U.S.-produced LNG vessel exports in Jan–Aug 2026 versus **3,458.5 Bcf** in the same 2025 period, a 21% increase. Thus Europe-bound volume declined slightly while total US vessel exports increased. Do not interpret either as proof of political leverage, realized profit, physical terminal discharge, or a cargo's actual final destination.

## October vessel-call reporting gap

The latest DOE monthly report and the accompanying LNG export detail spreadsheet cover **through August 2026**, not October. They cannot directly corroborate the October 27–30 Rotterdam vessel-call leads. A report's filing/reporting period must not be confused with its October 22 publication date.

DOE FE-746R vessel-export form requires departure date, destination country, supplier, cargo volume in Mcf, U.S. export terminal, export-point price in USD/MMBtu, vessel name, purchaser/end user, contract duration and heat content: https://www.energy.gov/sites/default/files/2023-08/LNG%20Exports%20by%20Vessel%20Form_0.pdf . The form is a **reporting template**, not a populated cargo record, and does not specify IMO as a mandatory column.
