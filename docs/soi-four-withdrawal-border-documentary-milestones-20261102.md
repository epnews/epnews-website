# Four withdrawal and border documentary milestones

These official records and identified research sources establish narrow status milestones, not comparable diplomatic influence.

## 2018-us-iran-jcpoa-exit

- Milestone: 2018-05-08 — us-announced-cessation-of-jcpoa-participation-and-reimposition-of-sanctions
- Roles: us: announcing-government; eu: remaining-jcpoa-participant; china: remaining-jcpoa-participant; russia: remaining-jcpoa-participant
- Limitation: JCPOA was a political arrangement, not a US-ratified treaty. US sanctions implementation and Iranian nuclear compliance require separate measurements.
- Source references:
  - https://trumpwhitehouse.archives.gov/presidential-actions/ceasing-u-s-participation-jcpoa-taking-additional-action-counter-irans-malign-influence-deny-iran-paths-nuclear-weapon/
  - https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/policies/jcpoa-iran-restrictive-measures/

## 2023-russia-new-start

- Milestone: 2023-02-21 — russia-announced-suspension-of-participation-in-new-start
- Roles: russia: suspension-announcing-party; us: treaty-counterparty
- Limitation: Russia's announced suspension is not identical to formal treaty withdrawal or an independently measured change in deployed nuclear forces.
- Source references:
  - https://www.state.gov/new-start-treaty
  - https://www.armscontrol.org/act/2023-03/news/russia-suspends-new-start-treaty

## 2017-india-china-doklam-disengagement

- Milestone: 2017-08-28 — india-announced-expeditious-disengagement-of-border-personnel
- Roles: india: disengagement-announcing-government; china: disengagement-counterparty
- Limitation: Indian foreign ministry statement is evidence of India's official account, not independent verification of subsequent positions or lasting resolution.
- Source references:
  - https://www.mea.gov.in/press-releases.htm?dtl/28893/Press_Statement_on_Doklam_disengagement_understanding

## 2024-india-china-lac-patrolling

- Milestone: 2024-10-21 — india-china-patrolling-arrangements-agreed-as-reported-by-indian-foreign-ministry
- Roles: india: agreement-party; china: agreement-party
- Limitation: Government report on patrolling arrangements is not a measured end to all border disputes, independently verified patrol activity or durable diplomatic influence.
- Source references:
  - https://www.mea.gov.in/lok-sabha.htm?dtl/39008/QUESTION_NO_843_INDOCHINA_AGREEMENT_ON_BORDERRELATED_ISSUES

## Measurement policy

No case receives a numeric score, claimed cost, net benefit or causal effect. Russian New START suspension is not conflated with treaty termination; the JCPOA is not labeled a ratified treaty; Indian government border statements are not treated as independent field verification. Existing meter/UI remains untouched.
