Source: [EIA August 2026 Short-Term Energy Outlook, Table 5b](https://www.eia.gov/outlooks/steo/archives/Aug26.pdf). USD per thousand cubic feet.

| Price | Q1 2025 | Q1 2026 | Q2 2025 | Q2 2026 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| U.S. residential delivered | 13.11 | 14.83 | 18.50 | 19.74 |
| Henry Hub wholesale spot | 4.31 | 4.98 | 3.31 | 3.07 |

Residential prices increased in **both quarters**, by +$1.72/Mcf (+13.1%) in Q1 and +$1.24/Mcf (+6.7%) in Q2. Henry Hub increased +$0.67/Mcf in Q1 but declined -$0.24/Mcf in Q2. These are official STEO figures published in August 2026, not a claim of finalized monthly billing values. No volume-weighted H1 average or household bill change is calculated because matching sector volumes are not yet available.
