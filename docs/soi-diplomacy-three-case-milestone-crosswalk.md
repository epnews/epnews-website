# Diplomatic milestone evidence crosswalk: US, Russia and India

The case register already contains **descriptive implementation milestones** for these three actors. This crosswalk retains their exact definitions and source leads, while explicitly separating observable milestones from independent proof of causal diplomatic influence.

## US — 2023 Camp David trilateral cooperation commitments

**Baseline:** 2014 Trilateral Information Sharing Arrangement and pre-existing bilateral channels; Japan-ROK GSOMIA restored before 2023 Camp David summit.

**Follow-up:** Activated December 19, 2023; continued operation publicly confirmed by July 2024 US-Japan Security Consultative Committee.

**Measured unit:** binary institutional operational status (preactivation arrangement vs activated continuous sharing).

**Existing source leads:** https://www.mod.go.jp/en/article/2023/12/c74496d9056f871004eb2983fb03d626fcc2ff94.html; https://en.yna.co.kr/view/AEN20231219001751315; https://www.mod.go.jp/en/images/b40703a6ef7d0822c278593b3172b4545b475b53.pdf; https://www.defense.gov/News/Releases/Release/Article/3852169/joint-statement-of-the-security-consultative-committee-22/.

**Outstanding verification:** Pre-existing US bilateral alliances and independent Japan-ROK reconciliation.; Identify direct defense coordination costs and concessions in comparable units..

**Disposition:** descriptive milestone only; attribution and actual net cost unverified.

## RUSSIA — Russia-Belarus Union State security guarantees treaty

**Baseline:** Russia–Belarus bilateral security guarantees treaty signed December 6, 2024 but not yet legally in force.

**Follow-up:** Russia–Belarus bilateral security guarantees treaty entered into force March 13, 2025.

**Measured unit:** binary legal status of the same bilateral treaty (not-in-force vs in-force).

**Existing source leads:** https://www.president.gov.by/en/events/podpisanie-dokumentov-po-itogam-zasedania-vysshego-gosudarstvennogo-soveta-souznogo-gosudarstva-1733500048; https://www.interfax-russia.ru/military/news/639921.

**Outstanding verification:** Pre-existing Union State obligations and Belarus's own security choices.; Identify financing, sovereignty concessions, and sanctions-related consequences..

**Disposition:** descriptive milestone only; attribution and actual net cost unverified.

## INDIA — India-UAE Comprehensive Economic Partnership Agreement enters into force

**Baseline:** FY2021-22 merchandise trade USD 72.9bn.

**Follow-up:** FY2023-24 merchandise trade USD 83.7bn.

**Measured unit:** nominal bilateral merchandise trade USD billion.

**Existing source leads:** See existing worklist source fields.

**Outstanding verification:** Energy prices, global trade rebound, exchange rates and UAE demand.; Measure tariff revenue forgone, adjustment costs and bilateral distributional effects.; Reconcile official historical bilateral trade value against later TradeStat revisions; separate oil prices from volumes.

**Disposition:** descriptive milestone only; attribution and actual net cost unverified.

The US case does not establish missile warning system uptime or uniquely American causation; the Russia case tracks treaty legal entry into force, not independently measured operational integration; India's nominal merchandise-trade comparison is exposed to energy prices, product mix, currency effects and revisions. No new gates are closed and no needle movement is authorized.
