# LNG exporter peer earnings and domestic cost-incidence audit

**Scope:** H1 2025 vs H1 2026, issuer consolidated financials, USD millions. This is a **descriptive comparison**, not a national benefit or disruption-attributable windfall estimate.

| Measure | Cheniere 2025 → 2026 | Venture Global 2025 → 2026 | Combined descriptive change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue | 10,085 → 11,600 | 5,995 → 9,177 | +4,697 |
| Adjusted EBITDA (non-GAAP) | 3,288 → 4,137 | 2,739 → 3,863 | +1,973 |
| GAAP net income | 1,979 → -434 | 764 → 1,835 | -1,342 |
| LNG exported (TBtu) | 1,159 → 1,360 | 564.4 → 965.5 | Not a national total |

**Primary sources:** [Cheniere Q2 2026](https://lngir.cheniere.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/343/cheniere-reports-second-quarter-2026-results-and-raises), [Venture Global Q2 2026](https://investors.ventureglobal.com/news/news-details/2026/Venture-Global-Reports-Second-Quarter-2026-Results/default.aspx), [Sempra Q2 2026](https://www.sempra.com/newsroom/press-releases/sempra-reports-strong-second-quarter-2026-results).

**Alternative explanation:** Venture Global attributes growth predominantly to Plaquemines commissioning and volume ramp-up; this is not proof of geopolitical demand shock. Cheniere's derivative fair-value movements materially affect GAAP net income. Sempra consolidated H1 GAAP earnings 1,367 → 1,833 are diversified utility/infrastructure results and **excluded** from the two LNG-issuer sum.

**Domestic costs — investigation status:** U.S. residential, industrial and power-sector gas/electricity price and quantity effects have **not** been causally estimated. Required next step: matching EIA monthly retail prices and sector consumption H1 2025/H1 2026, Henry Hub spot and production, storage, weather, domestic demand, LNG feedgas use; model an explicit no-incremental-export counterfactual. Observed price differences alone cannot be attributed to LNG exports, and a price increase can transfer income between U.S. groups rather than represent equal national resource cost.

**Interpretation:** +$1.973B adjusted EBITDA for two issuers is a measured change, **not** net profit, taxpayer receipts, a U.S. GDP increment, or proof of geopolitical leverage. Keep influence and net national economic benefit null. Keep meter editorial readiness 53%. No UI changes.
