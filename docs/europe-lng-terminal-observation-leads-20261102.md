## Why the terminal's function matters

Port of Rotterdam states Gate handles imports, LNG reloading for re-export, pipeline send-out, and truck/breakbulk movements (https://www.portofrotterdam.com/en/logistics/cargo/lng). Thus even an authenticated terminal call cannot automatically be classified as an import discharge. Verify direction of cargo transfer, original load port, intermediate transshipment, quantity, IMO and primary terminal documentation.

Fluxys' November 2025 LNG terminalling programme (https://www.fluxys.com/-/media/project/fluxys/public/corporate/fluxyscom/documents/fluxys-lng/commercial/agreements/terminalling-programme/lng-terminalling-programme-en--november-2025.pdf) describes ship berthing slots, storage and send-out as distinct services; a booked slot does not prove a vessel's completed unloading.
