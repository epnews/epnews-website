The no-joint-abstention annual series is included in the accompanying JSON. Baseline and no-joint-abstention values preserve directional 2021–2024 movement for all five spheres. Yes/no-only values change the level substantially, particularly for China, India and EU, underscoring that abstentions are analytically meaningful. The stricter EU threshold changes the set of eligible resolutions.

**Remaining limits:** resolution issue mix is not held constant across years; partner nonvoting/missingness and actor voting coverage vary. Topic-category standardization and a matched-resolution counterfactual remain outstanding. The sensitivity results are descriptive and cannot authorize a causal influence score. This audit does not change the existing main meter or its evidence gates.
