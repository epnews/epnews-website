# Verified DOE August 2026 LNG FOB price and volume comparison

**Source vintage:** US Department of Energy, *Natural Gas Imports and Exports Monthly, August 2026*, published 22 October 2026, tables 3a (report p. 6), 14a (p. 28) and 14b (p. 29). https://www.energy.gov/sites/default/files/2026-10/Natural%20Gas%20Imports%20and%20Exports%20Monthly%20August%202026.pdf

**Important:** All prices above are DOE **FOB export-point prices**, not seller cash receipts, realized company revenue, profits, delivered prices or net US economic benefit. FOB includes gas commodity and liquefaction fees up to export; shipping after the point of export is not represented. Figures are published regional aggregates, not cargo-level matched prices or contracts. Reported 2026 August vessel total is 514.5 Bcf; region rows sum to 514.5 Bcf. The table is same month year-over-year and same export mode, avoiding mixing year-to-date with monthly observations. Prices are nominal US$/MMBtu; volumes are gaseous-equivalent Bcf and **must not** be multiplied directly by prices without a verified heat-content conversion. Rounding and region definitions may affect comparisons. DOE treats Türkiye as Europe and excludes re-exports and ISO containers from vessel-only figures.

## Comparison interpretation
- Europe: FOB price +$1.18/MMBtu (+16.4% using rounded prices); volume +2.7 Bcf (+1.0%).
- Asia: FOB price -$0.70/MMBtu (-9.8%); volume +68.7 Bcf (+76.3%).
- Africa: FOB price +$0.15/MMBtu (+1.9%); volume -7.2 Bcf (-11.4%).
- Latin America/Caribbean: FOB price -$0.59/MMBtu (-8.3%); volume -0.8 Bcf (-2.5%).

These are **descriptive comparisons only**. Neither regional divergence nor higher Asia-bound volume establishes shock causation, realized receipts, exporter margin, US household impact or diplomatic leverage. Different destination mixes and contract formulas can change regional averages.

## Source-method check
DOE's [monthly filing guidance](https://www.energy.gov/hgeo/guidelines-filing-monthly-reports) requires exporters to report export-point FOB prices in $/MMBtu, cargo volumes in Mcf gaseous equivalent, country, terminal, vessel, purchaser and contract duration. Public aggregated reporting does not establish that cash has been collected or identify net proceeds attributable to a given destination.
