# Six-code UN voting sensitivity: agenda coverage and concentration

This audit measures **resolution coverage**, not voting agreement. A resolution can have multiple policy issue labels, so issue-code frequencies overlap and **must not be summed** to estimate unique resolutions.

**Across 2021–24:** 309 distinct resolutions have at least one of the six selected policy codes out of 358 recorded resolutions. 19 lack any issue code. The six codes were selected for annual support, not statistical representativeness or causal inference.
