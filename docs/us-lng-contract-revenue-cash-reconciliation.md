## Cash timing and contract qualification
Cheniere's 2026 Q3 revenue note discloses **$422 million contract assets** at September 30, 2026 versus **$424 million** at December 31, 2025. These balances are **not** an invoice-by-destination cash-receipt ledger. The company separately reports operating cash flow, investing cash flow, working-capital timing and future performance obligations. None of those line items establishes cash collected on August 2026 Europe-bound versus Asia-bound cargoes.

The 2026 Q3 note reports variable consideration earned under contracts included in the future-performance-obligations table of **73%** for LNG revenue in the nine-month 2026 period versus **67%** in nine-month 2025. These percentages describe the accounting contract subset and are **not** the spot-sales share, realized margin or share of all export cargoes. The note explains that Henry Hub indexation and future customer delivery elections affect variable consideration.

## Cross-source reconciliation — what cannot be joined
DOE's [August 2026 monthly export report](https://www.energy.gov/sites/default/files/2026-10/Natural%20Gas%20Imports%20and%20Exports%20Monthly%20August%202026.pdf) measures regional FOB prices and vessel export volumes. Cheniere's nine-month issuer revenue covers multiple terminals, contract types and recognition dates, includes derivatives, and does not report a matching region/cargo/cash-receipt split. A DOE FOB regional average **cannot** be multiplied by Cheniere's nine-month recognized TBtu or treated as the company's realized cash.

The consolidated Cheniere operating-less-investing cash bridge from PR #248 remains $1,221m (2025) to $2,439m (2026). It is not a margin reconciliation with the revenue figures above; working capital, capital expenditure, derivative treatment and financing matter. The 2026 revenue increase is not a net benefit to US households, national welfare or proof of geopolitical causation.
