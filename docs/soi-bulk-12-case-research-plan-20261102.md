# SOI twelve-case consolidated research plan

This batch tracks twelve cases across India silver trade, US LNG, EU–Russia energy, strategic implementation and diplomacy for the US, China, Russia, EU and India. The structured register is `data/soi-bulk-research-gates-20261102.json`.

## Required verification

Each case must have independently opened primary or corroborating sources, matched definitions, baseline, measured outcome, alternative explanations, cost accounting and source limitations. Source candidates are not verified findings. A signed agreement, UNGA vote agreement, tariff preference or export share alone cannot prove geopolitical influence.

## Parallel acquisition lanes

1. Trade and energy: India–UAE monthly HS silver flows, US LNG matched destination FOB/volume and financial incidence, EU–Russia energy substitution and consumer costs.
2. Strategic implementation: US–Japan–Korea warning data exchange, China–Solomon Islands police cooperation, Russia–DPRK implementation, India Quad mineral recovery operations.
3. Diplomacy: fixed-cohort topic-adjusted UNGA alignment for China, Russia, India and US; EU-27 cohesion as a separate measure; EU–Japan strategic partnership implementation.

## Methodology safeguards

Compare matching dates, units, origin definitions, product classifications and reporting scope. Separate FOB prices from revenue, margins and national net benefit. Preserve positive and negative outcomes. Require counterfactual analysis before causal attribution. EU-27 cohesion is not itself external influence. Differences attributable only to immaterial rounding need not block research, but classification or period mismatches must be resolved.
