# US LNG: cost–benefit decision gate (October 31, 2026)

## Purpose

Replace generic research progress with an explicit, reproducible decision on whether US LNG export growth has created **attributable net national economic benefit**. The currently displayed US Energy needle is **global LNG share 21% in 2024 to 26% in 2025 (+5 percentage points)**; that predates the 2026 geopolitical shock and must not be relabeled as a 2026 disruption effect.

## Reconciled issuer accounting — real, but not causal

Source: [Cheniere Energy 2026 Q3 SEC Form 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/3570/000000357026000038/lng-20260930.htm). All values are **US$ millions**, nine months ended September 30, same consolidated issuer and same reporting basis.

| SEC line / derived bridge | 2025 | 2026 | Change |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Operating cash provided | 3,484 | 5,124 | +1,640 |
| Investing cash used | 2,263 | 2,685 | +422 |
| Operating less investing cash (derived) | 1,221 | 2,439 | +1,218 |

**Formula:** 3,484 − 2,263 = 1,221; 5,124 − 2,685 = 2,439; 2,439 − 1,221 = **1,218**. This is a cash-flow arithmetic bridge, **not** SEC-defined free cash flow, LNG sales margin, profits, US GDP contribution, net national welfare or a 2026 shock effect. The issuer attributes much of the operating-cash increase to **working-capital timing**; more cash received is not necessarily more economic value earned. Financing, taxes, project-specific capex and capitalized interest are separately disclosed and must not be double-counted in the investing total.

## Counterevidence and identification

[EIA's summer 2026 gas analysis](https://www.eia.gov/naturalgas/) documents summer Henry Hub prices averaging **$2.93/MMBtu**, **6% below summer 2025**, amid changes in domestic production, inventories and weather. This observation does **not** prove exports lowered prices, just as higher exports alone do not prove a particular price increase. [EIA's H1 2026 LNG export analysis](https://www.eia.gov/todayinenergy/detail.php?id=68064) links higher exports to capacity additions, many of which were committed before the 2026 geopolitical disruptions. [DOE LNG export economic analysis](https://www.energy.gov/hgeo/articles/us-natural-gas-supplying-energy-world-august-2026-summary) and scenario studies must be labeled by scenario and date, not represented as observed 2026 household cost.
