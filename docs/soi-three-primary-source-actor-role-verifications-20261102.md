# Primary-source actor-role verification: three diplomatic events

These are narrow **documentary verifications**, not causal influence scores.

## US–China Phase One (2020)

The [USTR Phase One page](https://ustr.gov/phase-one) confirms both governments signed the bilateral agreement on January 15, 2020. A [USTR implementation notice](https://ustr.gov/about-us/policy-offices/press-office/press-releases/2020/february/ustr-announces-formation-bilateral-evaluation-and-dispute-resolution-office-pursuant-us-china-phase) confirms entry into force on February 14. Both are agreement parties; neither is uniquely responsible for the bilateral legal event. Implementation compliance and net outcomes require separate evaluation.

## EU–China CAI freeze (2021)

The [European Parliament May 20, 2021 release](https://www.europarl.europa.eu/news/en/press-room/20210517IPR04123/meps-refuse-any-agreement-with-china-whilst-sanctions-are-in-place) records a resolution approved 599–30 with 58 abstentions, withholding ratification consideration while Chinese sanctions remained. The [Parliament legislative status record](https://www.europarl.europa.eu/legislative-train/theme-international-trade-inta/file-eu-china-investment-agreement?sid=10401) distinguishes the 2020 agreement in principle from a legally concluded treaty. The EU is the institutional decision-maker for this ratification hold; China is counterparty and sanctions context. No investment agreement implementation or net diplomatic gain is inferred.

## EU–Japan Strategic Partnership Agreement (2025)

The [Council treaty database](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/documents/treaties-agreements/agreement/?doclanguage=en&id=2018023) lists the EU and Japan as parties, with signature July 17, 2018, provisional application February 1, 2019 and entry into force January 1, 2025. The [Council release](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2025/01/01/eu-japan-strategic-partnership-framework-agreement-enters-into-force/) confirms the final milestone. The US and China are not treaty parties and cannot receive credit for its entry into force from contextual mentions. No operational impact or net cost is inferred.

## Limitations

The documentary roles and dates are verified against official records. This does not amount to independent causal attribution, verified costs or cross-sphere normalization. All scoring remains disabled; no UI or needle changes.
