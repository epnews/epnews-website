# India–UAE silver trade diversion: customs reconciliation research (follow-up to PR #438)

Status: **source-verification and classification audit pending**. This is a research protocol, not a completed attribution gate. No meter, dial, score, or existing evidence count is changed.

## Established evidence already recorded in PR #438
- World Silver Survey 2026: estimated 2025 Middle East bullion exports 480 tonnes, down about 80% year over year; regional measure, **not** India–UAE bilateral customs flow.
- Same survey: India total silver imports 7,222 tonnes, down approximately 6%; UK and Hong Kong origin shares approximately 40% and 30%; these are industry survey estimates with potentially different product scope from HS 710691.
- Reuters (July 25, 2024): reported UAE-origin silver imports of 1,998 tonnes January–May 2024, versus 133 tonnes same months in 2023; immediate pause in CEPA clearances reported by an official.
- India–UAE CEPA October 2024 joint committee minutes: dispute over value-addition accounting; disagreement is **not** proof of customs fraud.

## New leads requiring source-level verification
1. WITS / UN Comtrade India-reported imports from UAE, **HS 710691 (unwrought silver)**, calendar 2024 reportedly 2,425 tonnes and 2025 reportedly 169 tonnes. **Provisional, unverified in this PR**: confirm reporter, partner, flow, trade system, classification revision, netweight units, completeness, revisions and direct source URLs before publication as established fact.
2. WITS / UN Comtrade India-reported all-partner HS 710691 imports reportedly 2,634 tonnes in 2024 and 657 tonnes in 2025. **Provisional, unverified**. Do not infer a contradiction with World Silver Survey 7,222 tonnes without reconciling bullion, grain, semi-manufactured forms and scope.
3. Locate and open Indian Bureau of Mines *Indian Minerals Yearbook* table showing fiscal-year UAE-origin silver volumes; fiscal years are not calendar years.
4. Locate and open Indian parliamentary answer about CEPA/IIBX silver imports and certificates of origin. Verify the question date, numerator, denominator, period and precise meaning of compliance findings.
5. Verify 2026 silver tariff and import-restriction changes against CBIC/DGFT primary notifications. Treat 2026 as a distinct policy regime, not as an extension of the July 2024 shock.

## Pre-registered reconciliation and causal checks
- Assemble **monthly**, not just annual, India imports by UAE, UK, Hong Kong and rest of world, with matching HS6/HS8 product definitions and reporting basis.
- Distinguish country of origin from consignment, dispatch, re-export and IIBX/CEPA eligibility; where possible cross-check UAE-reported exports.
- Align monthly tariff schedules (MFN, CEPA, effective rates and any other applicable charges), commodity price, rupee exchange rate, demand and silver product form.
- Compare pre-policy 2023–June 2024 with post-policy August 2024–2025; treat July 2024 as transition month. Use matched product groups and a counterfactual design before estimating tariff causation.
- Document volume changes separately from prices, nominal trade values, importer welfare, tax revenue, profits and geopolitical influence.
- Avoid inferring customs violations from value-addition disputes or attributing all Middle East export losses to India.
