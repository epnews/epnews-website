# Official EIA monthly H1 domestic natural gas prices, 2025 vs 2026

Published October 30, 2026. Primary tables: [Residential](https://www.eia.gov/dnav/ng/hist/n3010us3m.htm), [Industrial](https://www.eia.gov/dnav/ng/hist/n3035us3m.htm). Nominal dollars per thousand cubic feet (Mcf).

| Month | Residential 2025 | Residential 2026 | Industrial 2025 | Industrial 2026 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Jan | 12.44 | 13.96 | 6.03 | 7.20 |
| Feb | 12.97 | 14.95 | 5.92 | 8.43 |
| Mar | 14.62 | 16.16 | 5.68 | 5.27 |
| Apr | 16.17 | 18.04 | 5.31 | 4.80 |
| May | 19.26 | 19.73 | 4.69 | 4.19 |
| Jun | 23.21 | 24.09 | 4.66 | 4.36 |

Residential price increased in all six matched months. Industrial price increased in January and February, declined in March–June. An arithmetic mean of six monthly prices is **not** the volume-weighted price paid in H1. These prices reflect EIA onsystem sales and do not represent all gas volumes delivered through marketers and transportation-only arrangements.
