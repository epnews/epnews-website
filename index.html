HOW WE MEASURE THE GLOBAL POWER SHIFT ↓

EP News measures changes in influence against a dated baseline, not a nation's existing size or power. Scores are editorial indices, not scientific measurements.

DimensionWeightEvidence to review
Economic30%Realized investment, trade, production, jobs and net costs
Strategic/security30%Access agreements, deployments, production capacity, autonomy and costs
Diplomatic20%Agreements implemented, voting coalitions, bargaining leverage and durable policy shifts
Energy/resources20%Delivered energy/minerals, capacity, routes, dependencies and net costs

Each dimension is reviewed on a −100 to +100 change scale. Composite = 0.30 economic + 0.30 strategic + 0.20 diplomatic + 0.20 energy/resources. Categories must be assessed with dated baseline, credible citations, counterevidence, and documented costs; otherwise no composite score is published. A provisional score is never substituted for missing evidence.

Evidence confidence is independent of direction: High requires multiple independent sources and realized outcomes; Moderate requires a solid primary record with material uncertainties; Low denotes preliminary or incomplete evidence and is not eligible for a public composite score. All changes require editorial review. Rival spheres and individual allied countries can gain simultaneously.

Five presentation zones: strong loss (−100 to −60), mild loss (−59 to −20), no material change (−19 to +19), mild gain (+20 to +59), strong gain (+60 to +100). These thresholds are editorial design conventions, not externally validated statistics.

Editorial safeguard: No numeric scores are displayed until EP News publishes a sourced methodology, measurable baselines, assessed costs and evidence confidence. An unscored gauge does not imply a neutral or equal result.
