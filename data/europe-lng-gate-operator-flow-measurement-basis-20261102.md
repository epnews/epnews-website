# Gate Rotterdam: operator-published measurement and flow basis

**New primary source:** [Gate terminal — Operational data](https://www.gateterminal.com/en/commercial/operational-data/), checked 2026-11-02.

## Operator-stated facts
- Maximum vessel LNG discharge rate: **12,500 m³ LNG/hour**. This is a **capacity limit**, not evidence that a particular vessel discharged at that rate.
- Minimum terminal delivery rate: **139,000 normal m³ gas/hour**, subject to additional services allowing lower rates under some conditions.
- Terminal heel for three tanks: **30,000 m³ LNG total**, described as **10,000 m³ per tank**. This is an operator specification, **not** an October 2026 tank inventory record; the site separately reports a fourth tank in service from October 1.
- An **OBA (Operational Balancing Agreement)** is in place and, when active, can make allocation equal confirmation.
- Terminal nominations follow the transmission system operator regime.
- The terminal states near-real-time **quality determination of LNG in and gas out**, and **fiscal flow measurement of send-out gas**.

## Consequences for evidence interpretation
1. **Stock change is not vessel discharge.** The terminal has outgoing gas flows and operational balancing/nomination mechanisms; GIE daily stock deltas cannot isolate one ship's cargo without those additional records.
2. **Fiscal send-out metering is not the same as a vessel's LNG custody-transfer measurement.** The public operational-data page does **not** publish per-vessel discharge meter tickets, tank gauging sheets, quality certificates, gas calorific values, or ship/shore reconciliation.
3. **Do not turn the 12,500 m³/hour limit into a GWh cargo estimate.** That requires observed hours/rate, LNG density/composition and calorific value, and losses/boil-off; capacity is not realized flow.
4. **Do not apply the three-tank heel figure to four-tank October capacity** without an updated operator specification.
5. **OBA conditional allocation=confirmation** applies to its specified balancing arrangement; it is not proof that Gaspeil `gemeten` assignments are certified vessel cargoes.
