## Important new methodological implication
A **fourth tank becoming operational on October 1** coincides with the **Oct 1 GIE E-status inventory day** and the **shared 970 GWh Gaspeil assignment**. **Temporal coincidence is not causal evidence**: no source establishes that commissioning changed the GIE reporting methodology, created an inventory jump, or caused the duplicate vessel attribution. This is a targeted question for Gate/GIE, **not an explanation**.

The older 180,000 m³ net / 200,000 m³ gross figures cannot be silently combined with October GIE inventory to compute utilization. Do not infer new capacity by multiplying old per-tank figures by four; new tank dimensions and usable volume are unverified.
